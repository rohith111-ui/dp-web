DATA PIRATES

Data Pirates is the official Computer Science & Engineering (Data Science) student club at NNRG Engineering College, Hyderabad. We empower students to develop technical expertise through hands-on learning, innovation, hackathons, workshops, coding challenges, and real-world projects. Our mission is to build a collaborative community that inspires creativity, leadership, and continuous growth in emerging technologies.

About Us

Navigating Data. Creating Impact.

Data Pirates is built on the belief that innovation begins with curiosity and collaboration. We provide students with opportunities to learn beyond the classroom through practical experiences, technical competitions, industry focused workshops, and real world projects. By fostering teamwork, creativity, and leadership, we empower every member to develop the confidence and skills needed to succeed in academics, careers, and the rapidly evolving world of technology.
